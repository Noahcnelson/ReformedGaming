Major Reformed Standards

These documents shape doctrine, worship, and discipleship across Reformed traditions.

Westminster Shorter Catechism

Classic Q&A format for memorization, theology, and family discipleship.

Westminster Confession of Faith

Comprehensive doctrinal statement from the Westminster Assembly (1640s).

Heidelberg Catechism

Warm, pastoral teaching organized around guilt, grace, and gratitude.

Belgic Confession

Clear doctrinal witness authored in the Reformed churches of the Low Countries.

Canons of Dort

Responses to the Remonstrant controversy with careful soteriological definitions.

Three Forms of Unity

The Heidelberg Catechism, Belgic Confession, and Canons of Dort together.
